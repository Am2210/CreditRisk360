# Data quality and exploratory findings

The September 30, 2026 SBA 7(a) export contains FY2010–FY2019 approvals. The supplied 7(a) data dictionary matches all **42** CSV headers. The CSV is **255,300,948 bytes** and contains **545,751 source records**. DuckDB keeps one record per exact full-row group, producing **544,615 distinct records**. There were **886 groups** with repeats and **1,136 excess copies**. The excess copies were 824 cancelled, 293 paid in full and 19 exempt. `LocationID` identifies the lender, so this check cannot establish unique underlying loans. The source counts and checksums are in the [machine-readable quality report](../reports/data_quality.json).

## Dates, completeness and normalization

- All five date fields parsed without format errors. Approval dates span October 1, 2009 to September 30, 2019. First disbursement spans October 1, 2009 to June 17, 2026. Paid-in-full dates span November 1, 2009 to August 31, 2026. Recorded charge-off dates span June 15, 2010 to October 1, 2026, including two records dated after the snapshot. See [date coverage](../reports/date_coverage.csv).
- On distinct records, **66,227** lack first disbursement dates. Of these, **65,964** are recorded as cancelled or undisbursed and **263** are in other statuses. **510,071** lack a charge-off date and **147,187** lack a payoff date. These dates are applicable only to certain outcomes. Ten `CHGOFF` records lack a charge-off date.
- Chronology checks found **424** disbursement dates before approval and **5** payoff dates before disbursement. Three payoff conflicts are the primary eligibility reason. Two others have an earlier exclusion reason. Approval fiscal year reconciles to the federal fiscal-year convention for every distinct record.
- **231** records have zero term and **8** have zero initial interest rate. These remain visible for audit and map to null in the feature view. The maximum term is **847 months** and requires a business-rule decision before modeling. Gross approval is positive throughout, and no guarantee is negative or exceeds gross approval.
- Raw `P I F` maps to `PIF`, and the CSV encodes the revolving flag as `Y`/`N` where the workbook lists 1/0. The export supplies processing-method descriptions including legacy program names. They remain as source descriptions. Collateral is populated as `Y`/`N` throughout the release. Business age has **49,719** distinct-record nulls after mapping blanks and `Unanswered` to null. The full profile includes raw blanks, sample-inferred types and typed nulls for every transformed field. [Field profile](../reports/field_profile.csv)

## Recorded statuses and cohort

- Distinct-record statuses comprise **397,428 PIF**, **65,922 CANCLD**, **46,669 EXEMPT**, **34,554 CHGOFF** and **42 COMMIT**.
- **477,797** records are eligible and **66,818** excluded. Exclusions include 65,964 cancelled or undisbursed, 424 inconsistent origination dates, 263 missing disbursement dates for other statuses, 152 with insufficient follow-up, 10 charged-off records without a charge-off date and 5 with outcomes outside the observation bounds.
- **19,388** positives produce a **4.0578%** five-year observed charge-off frequency. This compares with **6.3447%** recorded as charged off at the snapshot across all distinct records. The measures have different observation windows and denominators. Among eligible records, **15,100** were charged off after 60 months and count as zero for the five-year target. [Target by status and eligibility](../reports/target_by_status.csv)

## Portfolio measurements

- The distinct records sum to **$205.37 billion in gross approvals** and **$152.41 billion in SBA-guaranteed approvals**, including cancelled and undisbursed records. These are historical approval amounts. Outstanding principal, utilization and exposure at default are unavailable.
- The [public portfolio summary](../reports/portfolio_summary.csv) omits segments under 10 records. The local DuckDB view retains all segments and reconciles each dimension to the portfolio. An empty eligible denominator returns a null rate. Geography uses project state. NAICS uses source six-digit codes and Census sector groupings. Industry codes have not been harmonized across historical revisions. [U.S. Census NAICS structure](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/year/2022/guidance/understanding-naics.html)
- The [origination-year chart](../reports/chargeoff_by_origination.png) shows the five-year rate and its denominator together. The 2009 cohort begins in October and the 2020–2021 cohorts represent delayed disbursement from earlier approvals. The [state chart](../reports/approval_by_state.png) compares gross and guaranteed approvals for the ten largest project states by gross approvals.
- California leads gross approvals at **$33.65 billion**, followed by Texas at **$20.12 billion** and Florida at **$11.82 billion**. In the eligible cohort, five-year frequency was **6.00%** across 128,046 records under $50,000 and **0.90%** across 49,807 records of at least $1 million. Frequency was **3.28%** across 345,165 records with reported collateral and **6.09%** across 132,632 without reported collateral. These are unadjusted segment comparisons and can reflect differences in program, vintage, term and borrower mix.
